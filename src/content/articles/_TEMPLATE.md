Files starting with "_" are ignored by the build. Copy this file, rename it
(e.g. `safe-medicine-storage.md`), fill in the front matter and write the body
in Markdown.

status:  draft     → not built
         demo      → built with a "Sample article" banner, excluded from sitemap, noindex
         published → live, in sitemap and structured data

category must be one of: Medicine Safety, Maternal Health, Pharmaceutical
Awareness, Medicine Storage, Patient Safety, Public Health, World Health
Awareness, Healthcare Education.

awareness: optional occasion name, e.g. World Pharmacists Day

Every health article must be written or reviewed by a qualified healthcare
professional and cite its sources before its status is set to "published".

## Supported formatting

Paragraphs, ## and ### headings, **bold**, *italic*, [links](https://example.com),
bullet lists with "- ", numbered lists with "1. ", and callouts with "> ".
